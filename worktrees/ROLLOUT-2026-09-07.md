## Audit limits

The auditor inspects registered worktrees, checks branch/path agreement and observes lease-file existence. It does not prove lease validity, process quiescence, ignored-data safety or merge eligibility. There is no automatic migration/prune. Counts are observations of a concurrently changing workspace, not a fleet-wide compliance certificate. Raw inventories and receipts remain in ignored local storage.
